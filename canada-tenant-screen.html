Privacy Policy

App: Canada Tenant Screen
Effective date: November 3, 2026
Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Canada Tenant Screen (the “App”) may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the App. The App is intended to help users search and review publicly available court or tribunal information relevant to tenancy matters and, where available, understand the context of published decisions.

Important: Public records can contain information about individuals. A name match is not proof that a record relates to a particular person. Search results may be incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, or refer to someone with the same or a similar name. Users must verify records using the original official source and must not use the App as the sole basis for a tenancy, housing, credit, or other eligibility decision.

1. Information the App May Process

  • Search information: Names, keywords, jurisdictions, case references, or other search criteria that you enter to locate public records.
  • Additional identifying information: If a feature allows you to provide details to distinguish between people with similar names, those details may be processed for that purpose. Provide only information that is necessary. Do not submit a date of birth, government identification number, financial account information, or other sensitive information unless the App specifically requests it and explains why it is needed.
  • Public-record information: Information retrieved from public court, tribunal, or other official sources, which may include names, case details, allegations, claims, outcomes, or published reasons for decisions.
  • Technical information: Limited information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the App, such as app version, request timestamps, diagnostic events, and server logs. The actual information depends on the services and logging configured in the version you use.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the App may be used to:

  • submit and respond to searches for publicly available records;
  • display, organize, and summarize public court or tribunal information;
  • help users understand the context and terminology of published decisions;
  • distinguish potentially relevant results from records that may concern different people, where supported by the available information;
  • operate, maintain, protect, and improve the App; and
  • detect errors, misuse, or security incidents.

3. Public Records and Accuracy

The App may retrieve information from public websites, court or tribunal databases, government open-data sources, or other third-party sources. We do not control those sources and cannot guarantee that records are complete, accurate, current, or available at all times.

Any summaries or explanations are provided for general informational purposes. They are not legal advice and may not capture every fact, procedural detail, appeal, correction, or subsequent development in a case. Consult the original record and a qualified legal professional when appropriate.

4. Information About Other People

You are responsible for having an appropriate lawful basis to search for, access, use, or disclose information about another person. Use the App only for lawful and fair purposes. Do not use it to harass, intimidate, discriminate against, publicly shame, or improperly profile anyone.

Do not assume that a search result identifies an applicant or tenant. Confirm identity through appropriate, lawful, and proportionate means before drawing conclusions. Avoid collecting or retaining more personal information than is reasonably necessary for your purpose.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Sources

The App may communicate with hosting, database, search, analytics, crash-reporting, or other service providers to deliver its features. These providers may process information on our behalf or under their own privacy policies, depending on the service. The App may also open third-party official websites; information you provide directly to those websites is governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the App, when required by law or valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service. Public-record information remains subject to the source's own publication and access rules.

7. Data Retention and Security

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to operational, security, and legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Search history, saved records, account information, and server logs may have different retention periods depending on the features enabled. Review and configure the App's actual retention and deletion practices before publishing this Policy.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for adults and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us using the details below.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You may choose what search terms to enter and should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information. Where applicable law provides, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions. Contact us to make a privacy request. Information appearing in an independent public court or tribunal record may need to be addressed with the organization that maintains that record.

10. Canadian Privacy

We aim to handle personal information in a manner consistent with applicable Canadian privacy laws. The privacy requirements that apply may depend on the nature of the information, the activity, and the province or territory involved. This Policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.
