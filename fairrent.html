FairRent Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Cakk TechSoft Inc. ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the FairRent mobile application ("FairRent" or the "App") handles information when you use our services.

1. Information We Handle

FairRent allows users to calculate a fair rent allocation among households sharing a residential property. Information that you may enter into the App includes:

  • Monthly property rent.
  • Property size and shared living area.
  • Bedroom sizes and room characteristics.
  • Household names and number of occupants.
  • Bedroom assignments.
  • Additional household expenses.
  • Rent calculation results and history.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not required for rent calculations.

2. How We Use Information

Information entered into FairRent is used to:

  • Calculate fair rent allocations.
  • Determine private bedroom and shared-space contributions.
  • Provide transparent rent breakdowns.
  • Display and manage calculation history.
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of the App.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by FairRent against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Information storage and processing depend on the application's enabled features and technical configuration. Please refer to the applicable application settings and disclosures for details about local storage and any connected services.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

We do not share information entered into FairRent with other users unless you choose to share it.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers where necessary to operate application services, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

FairRent may use third-party services to support application functionality, analytics, crash reporting, or other operational requirements.

Such services may process certain technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the relevant application functionality or fulfill applicable legal requirements.

Users should be able to delete locally stored application information by using available deletion features or uninstalling the App. Uninstalling the App may not delete information held by external services, if applicable.

7. Children's Privacy

FairRent is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Submit a privacy-related complaint.

To exercise applicable privacy rights, please contact us using the contact information below.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, our practices, or applicable legal requirements.
