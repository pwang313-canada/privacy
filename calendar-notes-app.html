Privacy Policy for Calendar Notes Effective date: October 24, 2026 Calendar Notes ("the App") is developed by Cakk TechSoft Inc. ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles when you use it. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App. 1. Information stored on your device The App is a calendar, notes, and reminders app. Everything you create in it — notes, reminders, and alarms — is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes your notes or reminders, and we cannot access them. Photos or videos you attach to a note are also stored locally on your device. The App only accesses media you explicitly choose through the photo picker or camera; it does not scan your photo library or upload your media anywhere. The App does not require you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or system calendar entries. 2. Permissions the App uses Notifications — to deliver the reminders and alarms you set. Alarms & reminders (Android 12 and later) — to fire alarms at the exact time you scheduled, as required by Android. Camera and photos — only when you choose to attach a photo or video to a note. These permissions are used solely for the features described above. 3. Advertising The App displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can manage ad personalization in your Google account settings: https://myadcenter.google.com/. We do not receive or store any advertising data about you. 4. Information sharing We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone. The only third party involved is Google (AdMob), as described in Section 3, which processes advertising data under its own privacy policy. 5. Data retention and deletion Your notes, reminders, and attached media remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data from your device. Because we hold no copy of your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. 6. Security Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security (such as screen lock and encryption, if enabled). No method of transmission over the internet is involved, since the App does not transmit your notes or media. 7. Children's privacy The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App collects no personal information from any user, as described above. 8. Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App adds features such as cloud backup, analytics, or crash reporting. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy. 9.
