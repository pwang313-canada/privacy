Privacy Policy

Dine Safe Checker · Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cakk TechSoft Inc. ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Dine Safe Checker mobile application (the "App"). The App helps users look up restaurant health-inspection information and understand whether a restaurant is shown as having passed or failed the applicable health check.

Important: The App is an informational tool. Inspection records may be incomplete, delayed, changed by the issuing authority, or interpreted differently by local authorities. A result in the App is not a guarantee that a restaurant is currently safe, and it is not a substitute for official inspection records or advice from public-health authorities.

1. Information We May Process

The information processed depends on the features you use and the way the App is configured. This may include:

  • Search information: restaurant names, addresses, or other search terms you enter to find inspection information.
  • Restaurant information: publicly available business details and inspection or enforcement records retrieved from relevant public sources.
  • Technical information: limited device, app-version, diagnostic, and network information that may be generated when the App communicates with our services or third-party data providers.

Do not enter sensitive personal information into restaurant search fields or other free-text fields.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • process restaurant searches and display relevant public inspection information;
  • calculate or present the App's pass/fail status based on the available records and the App's stated rules;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App and its services; and
  • respond to user inquiries and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Public and Third-Party Data Sources

The App may retrieve restaurant details and inspection information from government, municipal, public-health, or other third-party sources. Those sources operate independently of us and have their own privacy policies and data practices. We do not control when an external source updates its records or guarantee that information supplied by it is complete or current. Where available, users should consult the original source for the authoritative record.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be disclosed only as reasonably necessary to:

  • service providers that host, operate, secure, or support the App and its backend services;
  • third-party data providers when needed to retrieve requested restaurant information;
  • competent authorities, where required by law or where disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security; or
  • a successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of relevant assets, subject to applicable law.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services that the App uses.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including service operation, security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Retention periods depend on the type of information and how it is used. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information; however, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
