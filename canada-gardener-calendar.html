Privacy Policy for Canada Gardener Calendar

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Canada Gardener Calendar ("the App") is developed by Cakk TechSoft Inc. ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles when you use it. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information stored on your device

The App is a planting calendar and garden journal. Everything you create in it — your garden entries, task check-offs, and journal entries — is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes your garden data, and we cannot access it.

Photos you pick for the AI Plant Doctor or for garden entries, and photos you take with the camera in the Plant Doctor, are also stored locally on your device. The App only accesses media you explicitly choose through the photo picker or camera; it does not scan your photo library.

The planting calendar itself needs no network: the per-city planting windows are bundled inside the App, computed from public 30-year frost normals. No calendar request leaves your device.

The App does not require you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

2. Location and weather

The App does not use your device's GPS location. You choose a city from a built-in list of Canadian cities, and that choice drives planting windows, frost dates, and weather.

To show current weather for your chosen city, the App sends that city's latitude and longitude (from the built-in list, not your device location) to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com), a free weather service that requires no account or API key. Open-Meteo processes that request under its own privacy policy. No personal information is sent with the request.

3. AI Plant Doctor — no photo uploads today

The AI Plant Doctor screen currently does not send your photos anywhere: real plant-health diagnosis requires a plant-health API key (for example, Plant.id), which is not connected in this version, and the screen says so. If a diagnosis service is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before photos are uploaded to any service.

4. Advertising and analytics

This version of the App contains no advertising and no analytics. No advertising or analytics third party receives data from the App.

5. Information sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone. The only third party involved is Open-Meteo, as described in Section 2, which receives only the selected city's coordinates for a weather request.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your garden data, journal entries, and photos remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data from your device. Because we hold no copy of your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Security

Because your garden data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security (such as screen lock and encryption, if enabled). The only data transmitted is the weather request described in Section 2.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App collects no personal information from any user, as described above.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App adds a plant-diagnosis service, cloud backup, analytics, or advertising. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
