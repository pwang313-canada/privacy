Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vita Scan ("Vita Scan", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Vita Scan mobile application (the "App").

Vita Scan is a food scanning application that allows users to scan food product barcodes and obtain product, ingredient, nutrition, and related information. The App may also provide information in multiple languages.

Privacy at a glance: Vita Scan is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. The App does not require you to provide personal information simply to scan a product barcode and view available product information.

1. Information We Collect

The information handled by Vita Scan depends on how you use the App and which features are enabled.

1.1 Barcode and Camera Information

Vita Scan may request access to your device camera when you use the barcode scanning feature.

The camera is used to detect and read product barcodes. The App does not need to retain photographs of your surroundings or continuously record video.

Barcode information may be sent to our product-information service or third-party product databases in order to identify the scanned product and retrieve nutrition and related information.

If you choose not to grant camera permission, you may be unable to use the barcode scanning feature, but other available features of the App may continue to function.

1.2 Product and Nutrition Information

When you scan a product barcode, Vita Scan may process the barcode number and request information associated with that product.

Depending on the availability of the product database, this information may include:

  • Product name
  • Brand
  • Ingredients
  • Nutrition information
  • Allergen information
  • Product images
  • Serving information
  • Country or market information
  • Other publicly available product information

Product information is generally information about the product, rather than personal information about you.

2. Internet and API Requests

Vita Scan requires an Internet connection for certain features. For example, the App may communicate with external services to retrieve product information, nutrition information, translations, or other requested content.

Information transmitted during these requests may include the barcode you scanned, language preferences required to provide translated information, and technical information necessary for the request to function.

We do not sell barcode information or product searches as personal information.

3. Multiple-Language Support

Vita Scan supports multiple languages and may use translation services to provide product information in your selected language.

When translation services are used, text that needs to be translated may be transmitted to the applicable translation service. This may include product names, ingredient descriptions, nutrition-related text, or other product information.

We do not intentionally send personal information to translation services unless such information is included in text you explicitly submit or a third-party service requires it.

4. Personal Information

Vita Scan does not require you to create an account in order to scan food products and view available product information.

Unless a feature specifically requires otherwise, we do not intentionally collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Home address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Government identification
  • Precise location
  • Personal photographs
  • Messages or communications

If future versions of Vita Scan introduce account-related or other personal-data features, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Data Storage

Vita Scan may store certain information locally on your device to improve application performance and user experience.

Local information may include application settings, language preferences, cached product information, or other non-sensitive application data.

Locally stored information remains on your device unless it is removed by the App, you clear the App's data, or you uninstall the App.

We do not intentionally store sensitive personal information merely because you use the barcode scanning feature.

6. Third-Party Services

Vita Scan may use third-party services to provide certain functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Depending on the version of the App and the features enabled, third-party services may include:

  • Product and food databases used to retrieve product and nutrition information.
  • Translation services used to provide information in different languages.
  • Google Play services and Android platform services required to distribute and operate the App.
  • Advertising providers, if advertising is enabled in the App.
  • Analytics or crash-reporting services, if enabled in the App.

These third parties may collect or process technical information according to their own policies and terms.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of applicable third-party service providers for additional information about their data practices.

7. Advertising

If advertising is enabled in Vita Scan, advertising providers may collect certain information to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

Depending on the advertising provider and your device settings, this may include information such as:

  • Advertising identifier
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • App interaction information
  • General geographic information derived from IP address

Advertising providers may use this information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to their own policies and applicable laws.

If Vita Scan does not display advertisements, this section does not apply to the current version of the App.

8. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Vita Scan may use analytics or crash-reporting services to understand application performance, identify technical problems, and improve the App.

Such services may collect technical information such as:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • General usage information
  • Crash logs
  • Diagnostic information
  • Approximate technical location information, where applicable

Analytics services, if used, are configured to minimize the collection of personally identifiable information.

If Vita Scan does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, this section does not apply to the current version of the App.

9. How We Use Information

Information handled by Vita Scan may be used for the following purposes:

  • To scan and identify food products.
  • To retrieve product and nutrition information.
  • To provide translated product information.
  • To operate and maintain the App.
  • To improve App performance and reliability.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse.
  • To display advertisements, if applicable.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party service providers when necessary to provide a feature you request. For example, a barcode may be transmitted to a product-information service so that Vita Scan can retrieve information about the scanned product.

Third-party providers may process information on our behalf or independently according to their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or legal processes.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users.
  • Detect or prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the App.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Vita Scan against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Network communications used by the App may use encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS when supported by the applicable service.

However, no Internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the applicable functionality, maintain the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Information stored locally on your device may remain until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Third-party service providers may retain information according to their own retention policies.

13. Data Deletion

Vita Scan does not require an account for its core barcode scanning functionality. Therefore, there may be no server-side user account for you to delete.

You can remove locally stored App information by clearing the App's storage/data through your Android device settings or by uninstalling the App.

If you believe that we have personal information associated with you and would like to request its deletion, please contact us using the contact information below.

We will review and respond to valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

14. Children's Privacy

Vita Scan is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App without appropriate consent, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child in circumstances where collection is not permitted, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

15. Permissions

Vita Scan may request certain Android permissions when necessary to provide specific functionality.

Camera

Camera access is used to scan product barcodes. Camera access is not intended to be used for continuous recording or surveillance.

You can control camera permission through your Android device settings.

16. Your Choices

Depending on your Android device and the features available in the App, you may control certain permissions through Android settings.

You may also choose not to use features that require optional permissions.

If advertising is provided, you may have additional controls through Android advertising and privacy settings.

17. International Data Transfers

Some third-party service providers used by Vita Scan may process information on servers located outside your country of residence.

Where applicable, information may therefore be transferred to and processed in countries other than the country in which you reside.

We expect applicable service providers to handle information in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Vita Scan, our data practices, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to periodically review this Privacy Policy to remain informed about how information is handled.
