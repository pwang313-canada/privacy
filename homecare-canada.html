Privacy Policy for Home Care Canada

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Home Care Canada ("the App") is a home-management app for homeowners, developed by Cakk Techsoft Inc. ("we", "us"). The App helps you keep track of your home's details, maintenance tasks, expenses, records, and reminders.

The short version: everything you enter in the App stays on your device. The App does not create an account for you, does not send your data to any server, and does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.

Information the App Collects

The App only stores information you enter yourself, and it stores it only on your device. This includes:

  • Home details: information you enter about your home, such as its street address, home type, year built, and number of floors.
  • Maintenance tasks: tasks you create and track, including their status and due dates.
  • Expenses: expense entries you create, including amounts, categories, descriptions, and notes.
  • Receipt photos: if you choose to attach a photo to an expense, the App accesses your device's photo library only at that moment, and the photo is saved as a local reference on your device.
  • Home records: records you create about your home.
  • Reminders and in-app notifications: reminder and notification entries generated by the App's features on your device.

Information the App Does NOT Collect

  • No account data: the App has no login, registration, or user account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or password.
  • No location tracking: the App does not track, record, or share your device's location. A street address you type in yourself is stored only on your device (see above).
  • No analytics or advertising: the App contains no analytics tools, no advertisements, and no third-party trackers.
  • No payment data: the current version of the App processes no payments. The "Go Pro" subscription screen is not functional in this version and collects no payment information. If subscriptions are added in a future version, payments would be handled by Google Play, and the App would not receive or store your card details.
  • No data sent to servers: the App makes no network requests that transmit your information anywhere. There is no cloud sync, no online backup, and no account to sync to.

How Information Is Used

Information you enter is used only to run the App's features on your device — for example, to display your home details, list your tasks and expenses, show your records, and present your reminders. It is not used for any other purpose, and it is not shared with anyone.

Data Storage and Security

All information is stored locally on your device using the device's local storage. Because your data never leaves your device, no one else — including us — can access it through the App.

Deleting the App from your device deletes the information stored by the App on that device. There is no copy kept elsewhere, and there is no way for us to recover it after deletion. If you want to keep your information, make your own backup of your device before deleting the App.

Third-Party Services

The current version of the App uses no third-party services that collect your information. There are no analytics providers, advertising networks, crash-reporting tools, or social-media integrations in the App.

If a future version adds a third-party service (for example, a payment processor for subscriptions), this policy will be updated to describe it before that version is released.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for homeowners (adults) and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because all data stays on the device and no data is sent to us, there is no information for us to delete on request — but if you believe a child has used the App, simply deleting the App from the device removes its stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's features change. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a future version of the App changes how your data is handled (such as adding cloud sync or payments), the updated policy will describe those changes.
