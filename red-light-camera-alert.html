Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Red Light Camera Alert ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This policy describes what information the App may access, how it is used, and how it is handled.

1. Information We Collect

Location Information

Red Light Camera Alert uses your device's location to determine your position while you are using the App. Location information is used to determine whether you are approaching a known red light camera location.

Your location is used to provide the camera alert functionality of the App.

Depending on your device settings and the permissions you grant to the App, location access may be provided while the App is in use or, where supported and necessary for the App's functionality, while the App is running in the background.

Device Information

The App may have access to basic technical information provided by your device or operating system, such as operating system version, device model, and application version. This information may be used to maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

Personal Information

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, or home address in order to use the core functionality of the App.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the App may be used for the following purposes:

  • To determine your location while using the App.
  • To identify nearby known red light camera locations.
  • To provide audio or other alerts when approaching a known camera location.
  • To maintain and improve the functionality, reliability, and performance of the App.
  • To diagnose technical problems and errors.
  • To comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

3. Location Data

Location access is an important part of the App's functionality because the App needs to determine whether you are approaching a known red light camera.

You can control location permissions through your Android device's settings. If location permission is disabled, some or all camera alert functionality may not work correctly.

We do not use your location information to determine your identity.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your location information with advertisers for targeted advertising.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide or maintain services used by the App.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the App.
  • Comply with a legal requirement, court order, or lawful government request.
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of the developer, users, or others.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, subscription management, or other technical services.

Such third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

If third-party services are added to the App in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Location information used by the App may be processed temporarily to provide real-time camera alerts.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the App against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed specifically toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

9. Your Choices

You can control the permissions granted to the App through your Android device settings.

You may disable location permission at any time. However, disabling location access may prevent the App from providing camera alerts.
