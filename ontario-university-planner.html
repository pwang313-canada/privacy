Privacy Policy

Ontario University Planner
Effective date: October 7, 2026

In short: Ontario University Planner does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information, and does not transmit anything you enter to us or to any third party.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To make the app work, the following is saved locally on your device only (using your phone's built-in storage):

  • Favorite universities and programs — items you tap the heart icon on.
  • Your Grade 12 course marks — courses and marks you enter in the Top Six section.
  • Your program comparison list — programs you add to compare side by side.

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, backed up by us, or shared with anyone. Deleting the app permanently removes all of it.

3. Analytics and Advertising

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

4. External Links

The app includes links to official university websites (for example, tuition and fee pages). When you tap one of these links, it opens in your device's web browser, and that university's own privacy policy applies. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, the content or privacy practices of external websites.

5. Children's Privacy

Ontario University Planner is designed for high school students and their parents, which may include users under 18. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is no children's data to protect, store, or share.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the new version will be posted at this same address with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
