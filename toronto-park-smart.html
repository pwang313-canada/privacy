Privacy Policy

App: Toronto Park Smart
Effective date: November 3, 2026
Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Toronto Park Smart (the “App”) handles information when you use it. The App provides information based on publicly available Toronto parking-ticket records and may display historical patterns or estimates to help users make informed parking decisions.

Important: Toronto Park Smart is an informational tool only. Its historical data and estimates are not a guarantee that a vehicle will or will not receive a parking ticket. Always follow posted signs, applicable bylaws, and official instructions.

1. Information the App May Process

  • Location information: If you choose to use a location-based feature and grant location permission, the App may access your device’s approximate or precise location to identify a relevant area or street segment. You can deny or revoke this permission in your device settings. You may also be able to enter a location manually.
  • Search information: Locations, street segments, dates, or other criteria that you enter may be processed to return relevant parking-ticket history or estimates.
  • Technical information: The App or its hosting services may process limited technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service, such as request timestamps, device or app version, and diagnostic logs. The exact information depends on the services enabled in the version you use.

Do not enter sensitive personal information into search fields. The App is not designed to identify individual drivers or vehicle owners.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the App may be used to:

  • find the relevant geographic area or street segment;
  • retrieve and present public parking-ticket statistics;
  • calculate and display historical patterns or informational risk estimates;
  • operate, maintain, protect, and improve the App; and
  • investigate errors, abuse, or security incidents.

3. Location Permission and Control

Location access is intended to support location-based search. It should be used only when you invoke a feature that needs it. You can use manual location entry where that option is available, and you can disable location permission at any time through your device settings. Disabling permission may limit location-based features.

4. Public and Third-Party Data

The App may use public datasets or information made available by the City of Toronto or other public sources. Information obtained from public sources is subject to the terms, availability, accuracy, and update schedule of those sources. The App does not control those sources and does not guarantee that their records are complete or current.

The App may communicate with backend hosting, data, or infrastructure providers to deliver its features. Those providers may process information on the App’s behalf under their applicable terms and privacy practices. Review the relevant provider policies where applicable.

5. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers where necessary to operate the App, when required by law or valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service.

6. Data Retention and Security

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to operational, security, and legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
