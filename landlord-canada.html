Landlord Canada – Rental Property Management

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Landlord Canada ("we", "our", or "us").

Landlord Canada is a rental property management application designed to help Canadian landlords manage their rental properties, track rental income and expenses, organize maintenance requests, and manage important reminders.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, how it is protected, and what choices you have regarding your information.

By using Landlord Canada, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, the following categories of information may be collected or provided by you.

2.1 Property Information

When you use the property management features, you may provide:

  • Property addresses and locations.
  • Property types and descriptions.
  • Number of bedrooms and bathrooms.
  • Occupancy status.
  • Other property-related information you choose to enter.

2.2 Financial Information

When using rental income and expense tracking features, you may enter:

  • Rental payment amounts.
  • Rental payment dates.
  • Rental income records.
  • Property-related expenses.
  • Expense descriptions and transaction history.

Important: Landlord Canada is a property management and record-keeping tool. It is not a banking application or financial institution.

2.3 Maintenance Information

When using maintenance management features, you may provide:

  • Maintenance request descriptions.
  • Maintenance categories and status.
  • Maintenance dates.
  • Related photographs or attachments, if supported.
  • Other information associated with property maintenance.

2.4 Reminder Information

Information associated with reminders may include:

  • Rent due dates.
  • Maintenance schedules.
  • Reminder descriptions.
  • Notification preferences.

2.5 Device and Technical Information

Depending on the application's configuration and third-party services used, certain technical information may be collected, such as:

  • Device type and operating system.
  • Application version.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information.
  • Anonymous usage statistics.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information provided through Landlord Canada may be used to:

  • Provide property management functionality.
  • Organize rental properties and related information.
  • Track rental income and expenses.
  • Manage maintenance requests.
  • Create and display reminders.
  • Generate financial summaries and reports.
  • Improve application performance and reliability.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain application security and prevent misuse.

We use information only for purposes consistent with providing and improving the application.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by Landlord Canada against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Depending on the application's configuration, information may be stored locally on your device or on secure third-party servers.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.

Your information may be shared only where necessary for legitimate application operations, including:

  • Service providers that support application hosting, storage, analytics, or technical operations.
  • Legal authorities when required by applicable law.
  • Relevant parties when necessary to protect our legal rights, users, or application security.

Third-party service providers are expected to handle information in accordance with applicable privacy and security requirements.

6. Third-Party Services

Landlord Canada may use third-party services to support application functionality, hosting, diagnostics, analytics, or other technical operations.

Such services may process certain information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Where applicable, users should review the privacy policies of the relevant third-party providers.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the application's services, fulfill legitimate business purposes, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Users may request deletion of their personal information by contacting us.

Where information is stored locally, users may also be able to remove it by deleting the application or using available in-app deletion functionality.

Please note that uninstalling the application may not delete information stored on external servers.

8. Children's Privacy

Landlord Canada is intended for adults who own or manage rental properties.

The application is not designed for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is collected and used.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.

Canadian users may have rights under applicable federal and provincial privacy legislation, including PIPEDA and relevant provincial privacy laws.

To exercise your privacy rights, please contact us using the information below.
