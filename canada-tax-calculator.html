Privacy Policy — Canada Tax Calculator

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what Canada Tax Calculator ("the App"), provided by Cakk TechSoft Inc. ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used. The App helps you estimate Canadian income tax and take-home pay. It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other contact information.

1. Your tax information stays on your device

Everything you enter — salary, deductions, credits, and calculation history — is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party.

2. Free trial — device identifier

When the App opens, it sends your device's Android ID to our trial server to check whether your free trial is active. The Android ID is used only to recognise the trial on that device. It is not linked to your name or any other personal information, and it is not used for advertising.

3. Pro purchases — RevenueCat

If you choose to buy Pro, the purchase is handled by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your device identifier and your purchase history so it can unlock Pro and restore it. Payment itself is processed by Google Play. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, home address, or phone number.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or files.
  • No advertising identifier.
  • No analytics or crash reporting.
  • No ads, and no data shared for advertising.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only data shared with a third party is the device identifier and purchase history sent to RevenueCat, as described in section 3. The trial server described in section 2 is our own server.

6. Data security

All data sent from the App — to the trial server and to RevenueCat — is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

7. Deleting your data

Deleting the App removes everything stored on your device. To request deletion of your trial record from our server, email us at cakk.techsoft@gmail.com and tell us the approximate date you first used the free trial, or include your device's Android ID if you know it. We will delete the record and confirm by email.

8. Children's privacy

The App is a general tax tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
